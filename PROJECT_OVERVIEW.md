# GRANTLY Project Overview

## Objective

GRANTLY brings scholarship exploration, profile-based comparison, preparation and application tracking into one clear student workflow. The prototype is aimed at reducing the effort of comparing fragmented opportunity information and managing multiple application steps.

## Intended demo journey

A student enters the explicitly labelled demo workspace, reviews or edits an academic profile, browses synthetic examples, checks transparent SmartMatch explanations, saves interesting records, moves them into a tracker, and marks checklist items ready. Dashboard charts and counters recalculate from the current browser state. Funding Radar examples route to related sample records.

A separate admin-demo role previews local record editing and notice creation. It is an interface demonstration, not a security boundary or a shared administration service.

## Trust and privacy

The scholarship records and their dates, amounts, criteria, sample providers and notices are synthetic. They are clearly labelled and do not represent verified live awards. Official resource links are provided separately. Browser storage holds demo profile/progress only; no password or identity-document upload is stored. Income is optional. The prototype does not submit applications or claim eligibility.
